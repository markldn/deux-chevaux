# DEUX CHEVAUX — a Citroën 2CV crash-test film, lab and open world in one packed WebGL2 page

Target: one self-extracting `dist/index.html` under 64 KiB. No assets: car, dummy, world, sound and story are
all generated by code. Raw WebGL2 + soft-body physics written from scratch. `./run.sh` serves 0.0.0.0:9070.

## Research anchors (what "realistic" is checked against)
- 2CV6 (1979-90): length 3830 mm, width 1480, height 1600, wheelbase 2400, track 1260, kerb 560-600 kg.
  Tyres 125 R 15 (≈ 0.585 m dia), 3-stud steel wheels. Cd 0.51.
- Engine: 602 cc air-cooled flat twin, 74 x 70 mm, 29 hp @ 5750, 39 N·m, cooling fan in front, ahead of the
  front axle; 4-speed box behind it, umbrella-handle gear lever out of the dash. Spare wheel under the bonnet.
- Platform chassis, body bolted on. Interconnected suspension: front leading arms, rear trailing arms, one
  spring canister per side under the floor coupling front and rear. Inboard front brakes (discs from 1981).
- Body: ribbed bonnet, separate bolt-on wings, round headlamps on a bar, rear wheel spats, front-hinged doors
  (1965+), six-light glasshouse, flap-up front windows, roll-back canvas roof, tubular hammock seats,
  single-spoke wheel.
- Brief (TPV, 1936): two farmers, 50 kg of potatoes, 60 km/h, 3 L/100 km, cross a ploughed field with a
  basket of eggs without breaking one.
- **Calibration test** — German magazine, 1976, rigid wall, 40 km/h, 3-point static belts:
  2CV peak 33 g, static crush 375 mm, steering-column intrusion 41 mm, HIC 340, head 85 g, chest 23 g,
  shoulder-belt load 676 kgf. The simulator re-runs this test live and shows sim vs measured side by side.

## Physics plan
- Car = rigid body while driving; any contact above a threshold hands over to the node/beam soft body (node
  velocities from v + ω×r), which runs at 1 kHz until the car rests, then shape-matches back to rigid and
  keeps the plastic deformation as the new rest shape. Crashes accumulate.
- Beams are force-limited: compression yield < tension yield, densification below 40 % length, tear in tension.
  Energy goes into plastic flow, which is what sets crush depth and the deceleration pulse.
- Tuning harness in Node (test/calib.mjs) runs the 1976 test headless and prints peak g / crush / HIC.
- Replays record node + dummy state at 500 Hz, so every slow-motion angle shows the same crash.

## Tests offered
Rigid wall (40 1976 / 50 / 64 / custom), offset deformable barrier 40 % at 64 km/h, side pole 32 km/h,
side moving barrier 50 km/h, 2CV vs 2CV head-on, plane tree at speed. Open world: drive into anything.
